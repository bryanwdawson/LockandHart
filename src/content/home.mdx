## Before Seals. Before Legend.

Upper Clydesdale. Annandale. Service and settlement. The Lockhart name emerges
from land, record, and responsibility. <StoryTag t="DOC" />

The surname begins as **Locard** — Anglo-Norman, twelfth-century Scotland. Real
people. Real records. Not a romantic construction. <StoryTag t="DOC" />

## The First Bearing of the Key

*Inheritance becomes responsibility.*

Sir Simon Locard, Lord of Lee. Knight. Charter witness. Recorded in the 1323
charter at Newbattle Abbey. <StoryTag t="DOC" />

The story passed down: Simon accompanied Sir James Douglas, carrying the heart
of Robert the Bruce toward the Holy Land. When Douglas fell in Spain, Simon
recovered the heart and returned it to Scotland. <StoryTag t="TRAD" />

The Lockharts have always told it this way: Locard became Lockhart — keeper of
the heart. <StoryTag t="TRAD" />

The motto cut into the 1410 seal: *Corda Serrata Pando.* I open locked hearts.
